## RM Assessment

The revolver worries me more than DSCR. The utilization trend is linear, not seasonal; they are covering operating cash shortfalls with it. If aerospace slips again, the revolver becomes a problem before the aerospace business arrives. On aerospace, we have believed the recovery story twice, and it has moved twice. We have asked twice for the PO and received nothing, and the answer on the 1/23 call felt evasive. We cannot keep accepting verbal assurances. David is cooperative and does not hide bad news, but he delivers it late and without the documentation. The business is sound, and the auto headwind is sector-wide. This is not a management quality problem. If aerospace delivers as now represented and auto stabilizes, DSCR could get to 1.15-1.20x by Q3. I want to see documentation before underwriting that again. The credit has moved into territory where we need more information and more frequent visibility.

## RM Recommendation

I am not recommending a second waiver at this time. I recommend we hold the existing waiver and set a February 14 deadline for the following: (1) the aerospace PO, (2) 12-month cash flow projections with assumptions, and (3) names on the 60-90 day AR. If these arrive and support the recovery, I will come back with a recommendation to extend the waiver. I also recommend restricting revolver draws above 75% utilization pending review; utilization is currently 69%.
